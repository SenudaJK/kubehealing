## About KubeHealing

KubeHealing is a technical blog focused on running Kubernetes in production: diagnosing and fixing real cluster problems, preparing for the Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA) exam, and building observability and self-healing practices into cloud-native systems.

The content here comes from hands-on work with clusters — the kind of troubleshooting that starts with `kubectl describe pod` and ends a few `kubectl logs -p` calls later. Expect decision trees, command-by-command walkthroughs, and honest notes about what actually worked (and what didn't).

**Topics covered:**

- **Troubleshooting** — CrashLoopBackOff, OOMKilled, scheduling failures, networking and DNS issues, and other incidents you'll hit running real workloads.
- **CKA Prep** — exam-focused breakdowns of the topics that show up on the Certified Kubernetes Administrator exam.
- **Observability** — metrics, logs, traces, and the self-healing automation that turns alerts into remediation.
- **Tutorials** — practical how-tos for cloud-native tooling and patterns.
